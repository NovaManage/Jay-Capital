# Jay Capital Funding — Brand Assets

The monogram is the master J+C artwork already in production on the Jay Capital
portal (traced vector, not a bitmap). The J is the tall tapered ascender; the C
sweeps beneath it and closes into the J's descender.

  jcf-logo-horizontal-reverse.svg   horizontal lockup on navy
  jcf-logo-mono-navy.svg            one-color navy (embroidery, etching, 1-ink)
  jcf-logo-mono-white.svg           one-color white
